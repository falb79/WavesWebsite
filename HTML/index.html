Why You Should Try Our Cruise

Experience the charm of the Red Sea and Saudi Arabia's most treasured destinations on an unforgettable journey with Waves Cruise. With world-class amenities, luxurious suites, fine dining, and personalized excursions, every detail is crafted to provide an extraordinary experience.

Explore Our Destinations

Yanbu

Yanbu is a beautiful coastal city known for its stunning beaches and crystal-clear waters. It’s a perfect destination for snorkeling, diving, and enjoying a variety of water sports, making it a paradise for adventure seekers.

Amlaj

Amlaj is famous for its white sandy beaches and tranquil turquoise waters. This charming town is a great spot for relaxation, sunbathing, and exploring the nearby coral reefs, offering a serene escape from the hustle and bustle.

Tabuk

Tabuk is known for its rich history and stunning landscapes. Explore ancient ruins, enjoy outdoor adventures in the mountains, and discover the beautiful natural parks that surround this captivating city.

Jeddah

Jeddah is a vibrant city with a rich cultural heritage and beautiful waterfront. Enjoy the famous Jeddah Corniche, visit historic sites, and indulge in diverse dining options along the stunning Red Sea coast.

Neom

Neom is an ambitious project aimed at creating a sustainable city of the future. Located along the Red Sea, it promises innovative living, cutting-edge technology, and breathtaking landscapes, setting new standards for urban living.
